# GryphonCraft

Improves the default profession window of World of Warcraft Forever, without replacing it.

## Features

- **Shopping list:** select a recipe, enter the amount next to "Create" and click the bag icon
  with a plus next to it. A window, free or docked to the profession window, lists all reagents the recipes
  on the list need, how many you have and what is still missing. The number of crafts can be
  changed in the list. The bag icon next to the chat link button opens it at any time. Click a
  reagent to search it in the auction house; a button next to its search box opens the list there.
- **Your other characters:** GryphonCraft remembers the professions of all your characters. If one
  of them (same faction and ruleset) can craft a reagent, e.g. your miner smelting bars for your
  engineer, the shopping list shows it, and one click adds that recipe with the ore it needs.
- Options page in the game settings (Esc > Options > AddOns > GryphonCraft).

## Commands

- `/gryphoncraft` – help
- `/gryphoncraft list` – open or close the shopping list
- `/gryphoncraft list clear` – empty the shopping list
- `/gryphoncraft chars` – list your characters and their professions
- `/gryphoncraft options` – open the options
